Important Notice — Foreclosure

If you are in or facing foreclosure on a Massachusetts residential property, you have important legal rights. Under M.G.L. c. 244, § 35C and 940 CMR 25.00, homeowners selling property in foreclosure are entitled to a written contract with prescribed disclosures and a five (5) business day right to cancel after signing. Before signing any sale agreement, we strongly encourage you to consult with an attorney, a HUD-approved housing counselor, or the Massachusetts Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division. Purple Forge Capital is not a foreclosure consultant — we will not promise to stop a foreclosure or arrange for you to remain in the property after sale.
