A Note for Personal Representatives

A Personal Representative is a fiduciary under the Massachusetts Uniform Probate Code (M.G.L. c. 190B) and acts in the best interest of the estate. Some sales of estate property require a License to Sell from the probate court under M.G.L. c. 202 — particularly intestate estates and estates where the will does not grant the PR power to sell real property. Your probate attorney can advise whether that applies in your case. We coordinate with your attorney on timing and are happy to wait for any court authority needed before closing. Nothing on this page is legal or tax advice — please consult your attorney before signing any agreement.
