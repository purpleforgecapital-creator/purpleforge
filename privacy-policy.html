05 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. Vendors that help us operate the business — for example, web hosting, form processing, email delivery, SMS platforms, CRM software (such as Podio), analytics, and accounting. These vendors are bound by confidentiality and use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Affiliated entities and capital partners. If we assign a purchase contract to an affiliated entity or capital partner, we may share property and transaction information necessary to complete the transaction. Any such assignment will be disclosed in the purchase and sale agreement you sign.
  • Title and closing professionals. Title companies, attorneys, escrow agents, and other closing professionals as needed to complete a transaction.
  • Legal compliance. Government authorities, courts, or other parties when required by law, subpoena, court order, or to respond to lawful requests.
  • Business transfers. In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar transaction, information may be transferred to the successor entity.
  • Protection of rights. To protect the rights, property, or safety of Purple Forge Capital, our customers, or others, including investigating and preventing fraud or other unlawful activity.

06 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Site. These fall into the following categories:

  • Essential cookies. Required for the Site to function — for example, to remember your cookie preferences.
  • Analytics cookies. If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Google Analytics may collect your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URL. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising / conversion cookies. If applicable, we may use advertising-platform pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads) to measure ad performance. These will only be loaded if you have accepted non-essential cookies through our cookie banner.

You can manage cookies through our cookie banner (shown on your first visit) or through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse all cookies, accept only certain cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

We respect the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will treat it as a request to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to your inquiry, maintaining business and tax records, and complying with legal obligations. Typical retention periods include:

  • Active inquiries: for the duration of communication plus 24 months;
  • Closed transaction records: at least seven (7) years, consistent with tax and recordkeeping obligations;
  • Marketing opt-in records: until you opt out, plus a reasonable period to honor your opt-out;
  • Site analytics data: typically up to 26 months in Google Analytics, or as configured.

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

08 Data Security and Massachusetts WISP

We maintain a Written Information Security Program (WISP) in accordance with Massachusetts regulations on the protection of personal information (201 CMR 17.00). Our security measures include access controls, encrypted transmission of form data, password protection of business systems, and limits on who within our business can access personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information.

If we experience a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and applicable regulators as required by Massachusetts law (M.G.L. c. 93H) and any other applicable laws.

10 California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • The right to know what categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you;
  • The right to know the sources of that information, the business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom it is shared;
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information;
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information;
  • The right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information;
  • The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information;
  • The right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

Categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address), commercial information (property inquiry details), internet activity (Site usage data), and geolocation (general, based on IP).

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. We may "share" personal information (as defined under CPRA) with advertising partners through pixels and cookies if you opt into analytics/advertising cookies. You can opt out at any time using the controls described in Section 11.

You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We may require verification of the agent's authority.
